Preview: 

Sounds of naval battle between sailing ships. Cannons firing, misses splashing into the sea, hits bursting decks and exploding powder reserves. Attacking a fortified structure has hits crumbling stone/mortar and possibly drop into the sea. 

A collision between the ships with flapping sails has wood creaking and breaking, with one version having sailors cry out in anticipation. 

The destruction of ships comes in versions of two different lengths, both with several explosions and the sea claiming the burning remains of the vessel. 

 

This sound set was created as part of the OpenGameArt Winter Jam 2022 for the amazing game "War Ships" by Raghunandan M. A. - check it out here: https://raghunandan.itch.io/war-ships.
